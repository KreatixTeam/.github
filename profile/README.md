# Kreatix

Independent software from Finland, at [kreatix.dev](https://kreatix.dev). Everything is free to use.

## Products

- [Mate](https://mate.kreatix.dev) · A free personal AI agent for research, code, documents, images and scheduled tasks, on web, iPhone, Android, WhatsApp, Telegram and TikTok.
- [Kreatix Account](https://id.kreatix.dev) · One sign-in for every Kreatix app, with passwords and passkeys.
- [tunes!](https://tunes.kreatix.dev) · Upload music and stream it instantly.
- [Kreatix UI](https://ui.kreatix.dev) · A free, open-source community for discovering and sharing UI components and libraries.
- [Yapp](https://yap.kreatix.dev) · A walkie-talkie for friends. Hold their photo and talk.
- [Pigeon](https://pigeon.kreatix.dev) · Letters that fly at pigeon speed.

All products: [kreatix.dev/products](https://kreatix.dev/products/)
